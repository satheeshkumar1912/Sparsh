Inclusive Education

Belonging is the curriculum beneath the curriculum

Inclusive education at Sparsh means meaningful participation, affirming supports, and classrooms that flex for the humans inside them.

We reject the idea that children must “earn” inclusion by becoming easier to teach. Instead, we adapt environments, adult responses, and peer culture so each learner can contribute, regulate, and grow.
