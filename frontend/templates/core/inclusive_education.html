{# ——— WHAT IT MEANS ——— #}

WHAT IT MEANS

Inclusive education means every child learns together — with the supports, respect, and high expectations they deserve. At Sparsh, inclusion is not a special room at the end of the corridor; it is a way of designing every lesson, space, and relationship so belonging is the default.

{# ——— HOW WE DO IT ——— #}

HOW WE DO IT

Models that make inclusion real

Universal Design for Learning

Lessons offer multiple ways to engage, understand, and show learning — so barriers are removed before they appear.

Co-Teaching & Scaffolds

A special educator works alongside the class teacher, embedding quiet supports so no child is singled out.

Sensory-Friendly Spaces

Calm lighting, flexible seating, and regulation corners help every learner stay ready to learn.

Assistive Communication

Spoken, signed, visual, or device-based — every voice has a valued way to be heard.

Peer Partnerships

Structured buddy systems build genuine friendships and everyday practice in belonging.

Strengths-Based Assessment

We map what each child can do and loves, then build the plan outward from there.
