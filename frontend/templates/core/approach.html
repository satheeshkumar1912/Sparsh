{# ——— FOUR STEPS PROCESS SECTION ——— #}

The process

Four steps we walk together

01

Understand the whole child

We begin with listening — to families, to the child, and to the strengths and passions that often go unnoticed in a checklist.

02

Co-create an individual plan

Educators, therapists, and parents set goals together in plain language — communication, regulation, belonging, and academics.

03

Integrate learning & therapy

Speech, occupational, and behavioural supports are woven into the school day so goals reinforce one another.

04

Review, celebrate, adjust

Weekly conversations and regular reviews keep the plan alive — we celebrate progress and adapt as the child grows.
