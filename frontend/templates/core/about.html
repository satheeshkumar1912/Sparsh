About Sparsh

Sparsh is a new-age inclusive education initiative built on the collective experience of professionals who have worked extensively with neurodivergent learners, families, and educational institutions. Through our growing network of school partnerships and professional collaborations, we are creating inclusive support systems that are accessible, practical, and responsive to every learner’s needs. Our multidisciplinary team brings together special educators, therapists, counsellors, and inclusion professionals to provide individualised educational support, therapeutic interventions, classroom inclusion, teacher capacity-building, and parent guidance.

At Sparsh, we go beyond providing services—we build lasting partnerships with schools and families. Together, we create nurturing environments where every child is understood, included, and empowered to learn, belong, and thrive with confidence

MISSION, VISION & CORE VALUES

Sparsh mission, vision, and core values
OUR MISSION OUR VISION OUR CORE VALUES
  • Meaningful learning begins with trust. When children feel safe, understood, and valued, they are empowered to participate, explore, and grow. We welcome every child as they are—embracing their strengths, interests, sensory needs, and individuality.
  • Our educators and therapists look beyond challenges to recognise each learner’s unique potential. Working closely with families, we create a compassionate circle of support where every child feels capable, included, and confident.
  • At Sparsh, differences are met with understanding—not judgement—so every learner can belong, thrive, and discover their possibilities.
  • To create an inclusive world where every child feels valued, supported, and empowered.
  • We embrace individual differences and remove barriers to learning and participation.
  • Every child deserves the opportunity to belong, grow, and thrive with confidence.
