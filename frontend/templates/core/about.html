About Sparsh

Different abilities. Limitless possibilities.

Sparsh Inclusive Education exists so every child can learn in a place that understands them — not one that asks them to shrink.

Our story

Sparsh began with a simple conviction: inclusion is not a programme bolted onto school — it is a culture of belonging. From early intervention rooms to partner classrooms, we design for neurodiversity with warmth, evidence, and family partnership.

What we believe

  • Connection before curriculum
  • Strengths before labels
  • Families as co-experts
  • Every ability welcome

Looking ahead

This public site is the front door. Parent and institutional portals will extend the same care into everyday collaboration — progress, resources, and shared planning.
