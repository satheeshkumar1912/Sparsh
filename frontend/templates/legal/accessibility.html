Accessibility statement

Sparsh is committed to a web experience that works for as many people as possible.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This site includes skip links, keyboard-operable navigation, ARIA where helpful, captions on interactive controls, and a high-contrast mode.

If you encounter a barrier, email {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }} with the page URL and a short description. We will work to remediate issues promptly.
